Quiet Hour Press makes puzzle books and gift books for the quiet hour: after dinner, on the long train ride, on a slow Sunday with a pencil behind your ear.

Why loud?

Because a book you give someone should look like it was made for them. Our pages don't whisper. Every spread is designed on its own, in full color, with something to look at besides the grid.

How the books are made

Each title picks one subject and goes all the way with it. The first one, 90s America Crossword, is 48 crosswords about the 1990s, and every page is designed as a different piece of the decade: a cereal box, a video store tape case, a mall directory.

Every puzzle book has a full answer key in the back. The 90s crossword hardcover even comes with an autograph page up front, so it's ready to be signed and given.
