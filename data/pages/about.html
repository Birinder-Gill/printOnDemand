This book started with one question: how much of the 1990s do you still know by heart?

More than you'd think. The theme songs, the toys you begged for, the snacks in your lunchbox, the slang you'd never say now. We wrote 651 clues about all of it and built 48 crosswords around them, sorted into six sections the way you'd sort a shoebox of tapes: Television, Movies, Music, Games & Toys, Everyday Life, and Sports, News & Slang.

Then we gave every puzzle its own page. One is a cereal box. One is a video store tape case. Others are a mall directory, a karaoke screen, a dial-up desktop, a front page. No two look alike, because no two corners of the decade did.

It's made to be given. There's an autograph page up front for your message, hints printed upside down for when someone gets stuck, and a full answer key in the back for settling arguments.
