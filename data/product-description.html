Forty-eight crosswords about the 1990s: the sitcoms, the blockbusters, the boy bands, the consoles, the snacks and the slang. Every answer is something you knew by heart in 1996. The question is whether you still do.

In this hardcover gift edition, every puzzle gets its own page design, each one a different piece of the decade: a cereal box, a video store tape case, a mall directory, a karaoke screen, a dial-up desktop, a front page.

What's inside

  • 48 crosswords in six sections: Television; Movies; Music; Games & Toys; Everyday Life; and Sports, News & Slang
  • 651 clues across 24 categories. Every answer is a single word of 5 to 12 letters
  • 71 hints for the tough ones, printed upside down on the same page
  • A full answer key in the back, laid out like the cheat codes in a 90s game magazine
  • An autograph page up front for your message

Details

  • Hardcover, 8.25 × 11 inches
  • 80 full-color pages, one puzzle per page
  • Made for anyone who lived through the 1990s in America
